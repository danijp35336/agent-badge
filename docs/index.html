How it works

  • Free: claiming and checking in cost gas only. No fee, no token sale, no promises of returns.
  • Gated: only addresses holding an Agent ID can claim. One badge per holder.
  • Soulbound: badges cannot be transferred or sold.
  • Streak: check in once per UTC day. Consecutive days grow your streak; a missed day resets it to 1. Bronze < 7, Silver 7–29, Gold 30+.
  • Privacy: this site talks only to your wallet and the public chain RPC. No accounts, no servers, no seed phrases — you only ever confirm your own Claim / Check-in transactions in your wallet.
